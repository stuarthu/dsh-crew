## What was wrong

On dsh 0.2.x the Crew preset was missing from the preset picker, with no error. The
`preset-crew` row — the only home of the preset on that generation, because 0.2.x never
reads `$DSH_HOME/.agent-presets` — carried a row-level `disabled: !!js` reading
`ctx.get('agentPresets')`. A row's `disabled` is evaluated against the **loader** context,
which cannot see the registry service, so the expression was true on every dsh and the row
was disabled on every dsh. dsh keeps a declaration whose activation fails "on the roster
with its diagnostic", so the symptom was a missing preset and nothing else.

**The sentence the fix has to satisfy:** today "cannot read the service" equals "switch the
preset off"; after the fix, reading nothing equals doing nothing.

## What to do

1. `cordis.patch.yml`: keep the `insert` and the row's `id: preset-crew`. Change only
   `name:` to `'dsh-crew/host/crew-preset.js'` and **delete the `disabled:` line**. `config`
   — `id: crew`, `name: Crew`, `description:`, `order: 5`, the whole `plugins:` list — does
   not move, byte for byte.
2. New `host/crew-preset.js`: `inject = ["agentPresets"]`, three early returns (no service,
   no `register`, old folder-scanning registry), then `registry.register(config)` with the
   disposer kept as an effect, and a `catch` that reports one line rather than swallowing.
   It is a group carrier, as `@deepseek-ai/dsh-agent-preset` is.
3. `package.json`: `exports` gains `./host/crew-preset.js`.
4. `tools/verify-mount.mjs`: the pins are the **mirror image** — the row must be carried by
   that plugin, and must **not** carry its own `disabled:`. Scope the absence check to six
   spaces: a nested plugin row's `disabled:` (`tool-bash`, `tool-pwsh`,
   `tool-plugin-manager`) sits at twelve and is legitimate.
5. `CLAUDE.md`, `qa/gaps.md` item 63, `CHANGELOG.md`: state the new shape. Item 63 **shrank
   and did not close** — the wrong-context half is closed and pinned, the private-field
   half (`resolvedRoots` is not dsh's public contract) is still open.
