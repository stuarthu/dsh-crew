## What to do

In `host/roles-preset.js`, add `model.reasoningEffort` to the `agentOptions` built
from `roleModels`, only when the entry names a `model` and the effort is not
empty. Document it beside the `roleModels` example in the crew preset, in both
READMEs, and in an unreleased `CHANGELOG.md` section.

That new section goes on top of `0.10.0`, and two T-110 cases read "the top
section" of `CHANGELOG.md`. They were written while `0.10.0` was the unreleased
section on top, and they check the entry that shipped in it. Change the assertion
(ADR 0018) so they read the `0.10.0` section by name; the entry they protect does
not change.
